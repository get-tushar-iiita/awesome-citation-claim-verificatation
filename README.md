## Overview
Citations serve as epistemic anchors in academic literature, but an uncritical reliance on them obscures a pervasive vulnerability: citation-content mismatch. In these instances, a referenced publication is genuine and correctly identified, but fails to support the specific claim it is cited for. This repository curates resources addressing citation bias, amplification, invention, quotation errors, and the development of automated claim verification systems utilizing Natural Language Processing (NLP), Natural Language Inference (NLI), and Large Language Models (LLMs) to audit the scholarly record.

## Foundational Citation Distortions
**How citation distortions create unfounded authority: analysis of a citation network**
Greenberg, S. A., 2009, BMJ
[Paper / DOI](https://www.bmj.com/content/339/bmj.b2680)
Provides empirical evidence of how citation networks funnel attention toward supportive papers while ignoring refuting literature, creating unfounded authority.

**Academic Citation Practice: A Sinking Sheep?**
Rekdal, O. B., 2014, portal: Libraries and the Academy
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1353/pla.2014.0025)
Explores the systemic failure of researchers to verify secondary sources, leading to the endless replication of quotation errors.

**The Perils of Publication and Citation Bias**
The MIT Press Reader, 2021
[Article Link](https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/perils-of-publication-and-citation-bias/)
A discussion on how citation bias and the chase for statistical significance distort the established scientific record.

**Publication bias and the chase for statistical significance**
Nissen, S. B. et al., 2016, eLife (via PMC)
[Paper Link](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5904829/)
Analyzes the structural incentives of modern academia that exacerbate selective reporting and citation mismatch.

## Citation Accuracy and Errors
**Quotation accuracy in medical journal articles: a systematic review and meta-analysis**
Jergas, H., & Baethge, C., 2015, PeerJ
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.1364)
A meta-analysis revealing that approximately 25.4% of medical citations contain quotation errors.

**Assessing citation integrity in biomedical publications: corpus annotation and NLP models**
Sarol, M. J., et al., 2024, Bioinformatics
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae420)
Analyzed 3,063 citation instances across highly cited biomedical publications, discovering that 39.18% exhibited accuracy errors.

**Do they practice what they preach? The presence of problematic citations...**
Awrey, C., et al., 2021, Journal of Documentation
[Paper Link](https://www.emerald.com/jd/article/77/6/1304/434443/Do-they-practice-what-they-preach-The-presence-of)
Highlights that over 21% of citations in certain fields contain errors, acting as proof of citation plagiarism across sequential publications.

**Citation accuracy, citation noise, and citation bias: A foundation of citation analysis**
MacRoberts, M. H. & MacRoberts, B. R., 2018 (ResearchGate)
[Paper Link](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394539889_Citation_accuracy_citation_noise_and_citation_bias_A_foundation_of_citation_analysis)
Investigates the foundational issues undermining the validity of citation counting as a metric of paper quality.

**Giving Credit: How Well Do Librarians Cite and Quote Their Sources?**
Luo, L., 2020, Reference & User Services Quarterly
[Paper Link](https://journals.ala.org/index.php/rusq/article/view/6440/8511)
An empirical assessment of quotation and citation accuracy among information professionals.

## Automated Verification and NLP Methods
**Argumentative Zoning for improved citation indexing**
Teufel, S., 2005, Computing Attitude and Affect in Text
[Paper Link](https://cdn.aaai.org/Symposia/Spring/2004/SS-04-07/SS04-07-031.pdf)
Introduces the foundational methodology for categorizing the rhetorical status of sentences within scientific articles.

**SPECTER: Document-level Representation Learning using Citation-informed Transformers**
Cohan, A., et al., 2020, ACL
[Paper Link](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.207/)
Pioneered the use of citation networks to pre-train document representations to capture deep semantic relationships.

**Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims**
Wadden, D., et al., 2020, EMNLP
[Paper / arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974)
Explores the structural pipeline for verifying factual scientific claims against abstract corpora.

**scite: A smart citation index that displays the context of citations and classifies their intent**
Nicholson, J. M., et al., 2021, Quantitative Science Studies / bioRxiv
[Paper Link](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.03.15.435418v1)
Details the deep learning architecture behind ingesting millions of full-text articles to classify citation intent as supporting or contradicting.

**Scientific Claim Verification with Fine-Tuned NLI Models**
SciTePress, 2024
[Paper Link](https://www.scitepress.org/Papers/2024/129000/129000.pdf)
Evaluates transformer models fine-tuned on natural language inference tasks specifically for scientific text.

**Identifying the conceptual space of citation contexts using...**
CEUR-WS, 2019
[Paper Link](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-2414/paper15.pdf)
Analyzes citation context extraction through sequence labeling and text boundary delineation.

**SentiMedQAer: A Transfer Learning-Based Sentiment-Aware Model**
PubMed Central, 2022
[Paper Link](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8961296/)
A domain-specific model applying transfer learning to assess sentiment and context in biomedical question answering.

## Datasets
**SciFact**
- **Source:** Allen Institute for AI.
- **Description:** 1.4K expert-written scientific claims paired with abstracts from a broad corpus.
- **Application:** Rationalizing entailment in scientific abstracts and benchmarking scientific NLI.
- **Link:** [Kaggle SciFact Simplified](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/redzapdos123/scifact)

**PubMedQA**
- **Source:** Jin et al., 2019.
- **Description:** 1k expert-annotated QA instances answering research questions with Yes/No/Maybe based on abstracts.
- **Application:** Evaluating quantitative reasoning and biomedical logic.
- **Link:** [PubMedQA arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.06146)

**AVeriTeC**
- **Source:** Schlichtkrull et al., NeurIPS 2023.
- **Description:** 4,568 real-world claims checked by 50 organizations, annotated with QA pairs and open-web retrieval.
- **Application:** Testing multi-step reasoning and real-world context dependence.
- **Link:** [AVeriTeC arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13117)

**Sarol et al. Corpus**
- **Source:** Sarol et al., 2024[span_36].
- **Description:** 3,063 annotated citation instances across 100 highly cited open-access papers.
- **Application:** Training models for fine-grained citation accuracy classification and quotation error detection.
- **Link:** [Bioinformatics Paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae420)

**AVerImaTeC**
- **Source:** OpenReview.
- **Description:** A dataset for automatic verification of image-text claims, containing 1,297 real-world multimodal claims.
- **Application:** Advancing from unimodal to multimodal claim verification pipelines.
- **Link:** [AVerImaTeC OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=LA5NJPkUrw&noteId=7h40QNuKif)

## Tools and Libraries
**scite.ai**
- **Purpose:** A commercial smart citation index that displays citation contexts and classifies them as supporting, contradicting, or mentioning.
- **Link:** [scite: an AI Tool for Science](https://observatory.tec.mx/edu-news/scite-an-ai-tool-for-science/)

**PubMedBERT**
- **Purpose:** A pre-trained biomedical language model utilized in sequence labeling to extract citation contexts from academic text.

**BioBERT**
- **Purpose:** A domain-specific transformer architecture fine-tuned for assessing semantic similarity and textual entailment in biomedical literature.

**MedCPT**
- **Purpose:** A dense neural retriever used to rapidly isolate candidate sentences from source documents during the evidence retrieval phase.

**MonoT5**
- **Purpose:** A sequence-to-sequence cross-encoder model used for re-ranking retrieved evidence sentences based on relevance to the citing claim.

## GitHub Implementations
**allenai/scifact**
- **Implementation:** Data and models for the SciFact verification task.
- **Relevance:** Essential baseline for replicating scientific claim verification architectures.
- **Link:** [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/allenai/scifact)

**Andy-jqa/biomedical-qa-datasets**
- **Implementation:** Biomedical Question Answering Datasets.
- **Relevance:** Central repository providing access to corpora necessary for training medical NLI systems.
- **Link:** [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/Andy-jqa/biomedical-qa-datasets)

**SPECTER Model Repository**
- **Implementation:** Code and models for Document-level Representation Learning using Citation-informed Transformers.
- **Relevance:** Core embedding model required to map citations into shared vector spaces.
- **Link:** [ACL Anthology Reference](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.207/)

**MultiVers System**
- **Implementation:** Codebase for a state-of-the-art NLI model tailored for scientific claims.
- **Relevance:** Used to execute complex semantic comparisons and account for directionality and scientific hedging.

**ALCE (Automatic LLMs' Citation Evaluation)**
- **Implementation:** Framework designed to measure the fluency, correctness, and citation quality of text generated by LLMs.
- **Relevance:** Critical for mitigating LLM hallucinations during claim verification generation.
