## Overview
Citations are the epistemological foundation of academic literature, intended to trace the provenance of ideas and substantiate factual claims. However, the structural integrity of this system is frequently compromised by citation-content mismatch—a phenomenon where a cited publication genuinely exists and possesses accurate metadata, but fails to empirically support the specific claim for which it is cited. Driven by confirmation bias, academic negligence, and the misinterpretation of complex data, these quotation errors propagate unfounded authority and distort scientific consensus. 

This repository curates literature and tools focused on transitioning from basic citation authenticity checks to automated claim verification. It explores Natural Language Processing (NLP), dense evidence retrieval, and Natural Language Inference (NLI) techniques used to algorithmically determine whether a specific textual premise is logically supported by the retrieved hypothesis from a referenced document. The resources gathered here highlight current methods, benchmark datasets (like SciFact and AVeriTeC), and the persistent challenges of multi-hop reasoning and scientific hedging in academic prose.

## Survey and Review Papers
**Scientific Fact-Checking: A Survey of Resources and Approaches**
Vladika, J., & Matthes, F., 2023, ACL Findings
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.418/)
A comprehensive review of modern NLP pipelines, datasets, and methodologies specifically designed for verifying scientific claims against source literature.

**A Survey on Automated Fact-Checking**
Guo, Z., Schlichtkrull, M., & Vlachos, A., 2022, Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2022.tacl-1.11/)
An authoritative overview of the architectures used for evidence retrieval and textual entailment in automated fact-checking.

**Quotation accuracy in medical journal articles: a systematic review and meta-analysis**
Jergas, H., & Baethge, C., 2015, PeerJ
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.1364)
A meta-analysis proving that over a quarter of medical citations contain quotation errors, failing to substantiate the citing statement.

**What do citation counts measure? A review of studies on citing behavior**
Bornmann, L., & Daniel, H. D., 2008, Journal of Documentation
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1108/00220410810844150)
Explores the sociological and rhetorical reasons authors cite papers, distinct from actual evidence support.

## Foundational Citation Analysis
**How citation distortions create unfounded authority: analysis of a citation network**
Greenberg, S. A., 2009, BMJ
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.b2680)
A landmark study empirically demonstrating how selective citation and amplification distort the scientific record regarding Alzheimer's research.

**Academic Citation Practice: A Sinking Sheep?**
Rekdal, O. B., 2014, portal: Libraries and the Academy
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1353/pla.2014.0025)
A highly influential paper illustrating the academic "herd mentality" that leads to the unchecked replication of quotation errors.

**Argumentative Zoning for improved citation indexing**
Teufel, S., 2005, Computing Attitude and Affect in Text
[Paper / DOI](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/1-4020-4102-0_15)
The foundational framework for categorizing the rhetorical intent of a citation context (citance) for downstream computational analysis.

**Problems of citation analysis**
MacRoberts, M. H., & MacRoberts, B. R., 1996, Scientometrics
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02016205)
Early warnings regarding the uncritical reliance on citations as purely objective metrics of evidence.

**SciBERT: A Pretrained Language Model for Scientific Text**
Beltagy, I., Lo, K., & Cohan, A., 2019, EMNLP
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1371/)
Introduces the foundational domain-specific transformer model used in nearly all modern scientific claim verification tasks.

## Recent Research and Methods
**SPECTER: Document-level Representation Learning using Citation-informed Transformers**
Cohan, A., et al., 2020, ACL
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.207/)
Pioneers the use of the citation network to pre-train document representations, crucial for dense evidence retrieval.

**MultiVers: A Multiplexed Verification System for Scientific Claims**
Wadden, D., et al., 2022, NAACL
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.122/)
Proposes a multi-task framework that jointly trains evidence retrieval and claim verification to improve precision.

**Assessing citation integrity in biomedical publications: corpus annotation and NLP models**
Sarol, M. J., et al., 2024, Bioinformatics
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae420)
Analyzes thousands of biomedical citations to classify fine-grained quotation errors using state-of-the-art NLP models.

**Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims**
Wadden, D., et al., 2020, EMNLP
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/)
Introduces the VeriSci architecture, standardizing the modern pipeline of rationale selection and entailment prediction.

**scite: A smart citation index that displays the context of citations and classifies their intent**
Nicholson, J. M., et al., 2021, Quantitative Science Studies
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/qss_a_00146)
Describes the deep learning architecture behind the first commercial scale system for evaluating citation-content intent.

**Generating Scientific Claims for Zero-Shot Scientific Fact Checking**
Wright, D., et al., 2022, ACL
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.174/)
Explores synthetic claim generation to train verification models without the need for extensive human annotation.

**Scientific Claim Verification with MonoT5**
Pradeep, R., et al., 2021, EMNLP
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.233/)
Demonstrates the efficacy of cross-encoder ranking models in isolating highly relevant evidentiary sentences from long academic PDFs.

**Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations**
Gao, T., et al., 2023, EMNLP
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.405/)
Investigates the capabilities and limitations of LLMs in actively grounding generated text in verified source evidence.

**Evidence Inference 2.0: More Data, Better Models**
DeYoung, J., et al., 2020, BioNLP
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2020.bionlp-1.13/)
Advances the extraction of PICO (Population, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome) frames to automatically verify clinical trial claims.

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks**
Lewis, P., et al., 2020, NeurIPS
[Paper / DOI](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/6b493230205f780e1bc26945df7481e5-Paper.pdf)
The foundational RAG paper outlining how parametric models can be forced to adhere to non-parametric external evidence.

**Towards verifiable generation: A benchmark for knowledge-aware language models**
Liu, J., et al., 2023, arXiv
[Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05634)
Evaluates how effectively modern language models can cite evidence accurately without hallucinating or distorting the source.

## Datasets and Benchmarks
**SciFact Dataset**
- **Source:** Allen Institute for AI
- **Description:** 1.4K expert-written scientific claims paired with abstracts, annotated for entailment and contradiction.
- **Application:** Benchmarking automated scientific fact-checking pipelines.
- **Link:** [SciFact](https://github.com/allenai/scifact)

**AVeriTeC**
- **Source:** Schlichtkrull et al., NeurIPS 2023
- **Description:** 4,568 real-world claims annotated with question-answer pairs requiring multi-hop reasoning against evidence retrieved from the open web.
- **Application:** Evaluating systems on real-world context dependence and temporal leakage.
- **Link:** [AVeriTeC](https://fever.ai/2024/task.html)

**PubMedQA**
- **Source:** Jin et al., EMNLP 2019
- **Description:** A biomedical QA dataset designed to answer research questions with yes/no/maybe based on specific abstracts.
- **Application:** Evaluating quantitative reasoning and biomedical logic in evidence entailment.
- **Link:** [PubMedQA](https://pubmedqa.github.io/)

**FEVER (Fact Extraction and VERification)**
- **Source:** Thorne et al., NAACL 2018
- **Description:** A massive dataset of 185,445 claims generated by altering sentences from Wikipedia and verifying them without prior context.
- **Application:** Training baseline Natural Language Inference (NLI) classification models.
- **Link:** [FEVER](https://fever.ai/)

## Tools and Libraries
**scite.ai**
- **Purpose:** A commercial tool utilizing deep learning to display the rhetorical context of citations, classifying them as supporting, mentioning, or contrasting.
- **Link:** [scite.ai](https://scite.ai/)

**Hugging Face Transformers**
- **Purpose:** The industry-standard library providing access to pre-trained NLP models (like SciBERT and BioBERT) essential for citation classification tasks.
- **Link:** [Transformers](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/index)

**Pyserini**
- **Purpose:** A Python toolkit for reproducible information retrieval research, vital for the evidence retrieval phase of claim verification.
- **Link:** [Pyserini](https://github.com/castorini/pyserini)

**BeIR (Benchmarking IR)**
- **Purpose:** A heterogeneous benchmark for zero-shot evaluation of information retrieval models, utilized to test dense retrievers on scientific corpora.
- **Link:** [BeIR](https://github.com/beir-cellar/beir)

**Haystack by deepset**
- **Purpose:** An end-to-end NLP framework for building intelligent search pipelines, RAG implementations, and automated verification agents.
- **Link:** [Haystack](https://haystack.deepset.ai/)

## GitHub Implementations
**allenai/scifact**
- **Implementation:** Official repository containing the SciFact dataset and the baseline VeriSci model architecture.
- **Relevance:** Essential starting point for reproducing scientific claim verification experiments.

**allenai/specter**
- **Implementation:** Official code and pre-trained weights for the SPECTER document-level embedding model.
- **Relevance:** Used to map citations and claims into a shared vector space for semantic similarity scoring.

**allenai/multivers**
- **Implementation:** The codebase for the MultiVers multiplexed verification system.
- **Relevance:** Demonstrates how to jointly train evidence retrieval and entailment for improved accuracy.

**fever-ai/fever**
- **Implementation:** The official repository for the FEVER benchmark and baseline models.
- **Relevance:** Foundational implementation for open-domain fact-checking and pipeline architecture.

**castorini/pygaggle**
- **Implementation:** A collection of neural ranking models utilizing sequence-to-sequence architectures (like MonoT5).
- **Relevance:** Implements the cross-encoder re-ranking step critical for isolating evidence sentences from academic documents.
